Initial preflight on October 15 was blocked: no GPU was visible, the cgroup was near its 2 GiB cap, and disk free space was about 12 GiB. Resources were restored and then disk was expanded. Before formal stage A, `/root/autodl-tmp` measured 200 GB total with 32 GB free (85% used). The research environment sees an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 D with 24,564 MiB total; about 24,081 MiB was free at idle. The cgroup limit is about 80 GiB; formal runs observed about 16-19 GiB current use. Both valid stage-A runs used `/root/miniconda3/envs/card/bin/python`, Torch 1.8.0+cu111, with CUDA available. Disk remained above 30 GB free after both runs and diagnostics.

The intended research interpreter `/root/miniconda3/envs/card/bin/python` is Python 3.8.20; package metadata confirms PyTorch `1.8.0+cu111`, `pycocoevalcap 1.2`, and `pycocotools 2.0.7`, matching the recorded validated runtime's main versions. A separate default `python` resolves to a different Torch build (`1.10.0+cu113`). The launcher now exports the intended `card` interpreter to its training child. The valid C0/N1 runs exercised the complete BLEU-4, METEOR, ROUGE-L, CIDEr, and SPICE scorer chain on all 1,333 CC validation IDs at each registered point and repeated selected checkpoint. `/usr/bin/java`, METEOR 1.5 and SPICE jars were present and usable. No environment upgrade was attempted.

LEVIR-CC and LEVIR-MCI data roots exist under `/root/autodl-tmp/z1zy1`; each has captions, splits, vocabulary, labels, image/features trees, and semantic/pseudo-mask inputs. The CC validation feature/image directories contain 2,876 files each, CC pseudo-mask validation contains 1,438 files, and MCI validation contains 2,666 feature files and 5,333 image files. Main annotation/reference, split, vocabulary, and label identities are recorded in `EXPLORATORY_PROTOCOL.json`. Each of the 20 valid stage-A grid prediction files and both selected-repeat files was independently checked against the 1,333 CC validation IDs: no missing, duplicate, or extra IDs. Full feature and semantic trees were not independently rehashed. References were not changed. The source's normal semantic class derivation remains enabled.

Status: GPU, memory, dependencies, data, full validation references, and the five-metric scorer were usable for the valid stage-A pair.
